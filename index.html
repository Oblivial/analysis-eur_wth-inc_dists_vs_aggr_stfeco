ESS and WID Descriptive Analysis

This report compares country-year summaries of satisfaction with the state of the economy (ESS stfeco) with distributional series from the World Inequality Database (WID). Only years available in ESS and in both WID concepts are plotted for a country.

Data Availability

Common Countries by Year

The bar height shows how many countries have ESS data and both WID series available in each year.

Common Years by Country

The bar length shows the number of years with matched ESS and WID data for each country.

WID Distribution Change

This section summarizes annualized distribution changes for WID countries that also have ESS country-year data. Years are grouped into two-year ESS rounds anchored at 2002; the earliest matched observation in each round is used. Each heatmap column compares consecutive selected ESS observations, skipping intervening WID years. WID post-tax national-income shares are used where available, with pre-tax shares as a fallback. For each transition, the score is the mean absolute change across the nine cumulative decile-share cutoffs, divided by the gap between ESS observations. It is a magnitude in percentage points per year; it does not indicate whether inequality rose or fell. The metric uses the cumulative share profile directly, so it also applies when net wealth shares are negative.

Country and Year Change Heatmaps

Darker cells indicate larger annualized distribution changes. Countries are sorted by median activity within each panel. An x marks a transition whose previous or current year is within a WID metadata-declared extrapolation interval. Interpolation is not separately flagged in the available series metadata.

Wealth and Income Change by Country

Each point is a country, positioned by its median annualized change for wealth and income. Larger values mean more movement in the cumulative share profile; no direction is implied.

Largest WID Country-Year Changes

The top transitions are ranked by annualized magnitude and use consecutive ESS observation years as endpoints. An asterisk indicates that one endpoint falls within a WID metadata-declared extrapolation interval.

ESS Summary Change

ESS changes are calculated independently of WID. The mean measures movement in average satisfaction with the state of the economy; the IQR measures change in spread. Both are annualized absolute changes in ESS scale points. Years are grouped into two-year ESS rounds anchored at 2002, using the earliest available observation in each round. Heatmap columns label the selected endpoint pairs. Unreliable or unknown endpoints appear as blank cells.

Country and Year Change Heatmaps

Darker cells indicate larger annualized changes in the ESS mean or IQR. Countries are sorted by median activity within each panel. Cells are blank if either endpoint is flagged unreliable or its reliability is unknown; IQR cells are also blank when an endpoint IQR is unavailable.

Largest ESS Mean and IQR Changes

The largest mean changes are ranked by annualized magnitude. The IQR panel shows the observed IQR at both ESS endpoints for the largest annualized IQR changes, making the before/after spread visible. Segment color indicates whether the IQR widened or narrowed; an asterisk marks an unreliable or unclassified endpoint.

Country Time Series

The ESS figure shows the mean, median, and mode of satisfaction with the state of the economy. Open points mark country-years flagged as unreliable in the ESS summary file. The WID figures track the shares held by the bottom 50%, middle 40%, and top 10% for net personal wealth and national income. Post-tax national income is used where available, with pre-tax national income as a fallback. The group shares are summed from WID’s decile data, making the distribution easier to compare over time without mixing country currencies. Net wealth shares may be negative for groups with debt.
